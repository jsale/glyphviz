# Cat Scan Synesthesia — GlyphViz music synesthesia

One master glyph, 64 frequency-band hyperglyphs (4,529 nodes total) on a
Fibonacci shell — bass at the south pole, treble at the north. Four frequency
zones with distinct geometry, palettes, and motion personalities; five
animated signals per band including `rotate_rate_z` pinwheels and
`translate_rate_x/y` orbits. Change the master root's topology (node 1) to
rearrange all 64 bands at once. See `generate_cat_scan_synesthesia.py`'s
docstring and the companion notebook for the full design.

**The source recording (Tangerine Dream, *Cat Scan*) is not included**
(commercial copyright). All generated files are committed, so the animation
plays fine; to also hear the music in sync, supply
`media/cat_scan_all_mono.wav` (mono 16-bit PCM WAV, made from your own copy of
the track).

Load: **File > Open Node CSV** → `Cat_Scan_Synesthesia_Example_gv_node.csv`,
press Play in the Channels panel.
