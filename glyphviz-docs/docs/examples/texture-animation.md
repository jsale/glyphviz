# Texture Animation

A Channels track steps a glyph's `texture_id` through a numbered image sequence — flipbook animation played on the surface of a 3D object, alongside a video texture.

![Texture Animation loaded in GlyphViz](../assets/examples/texture-animation.png)

**Try it:** [download the examples set](../getting-started/installation.md#example-scenes), then open `Texture_Animation_Example/texture_animation_gv_node.csv` — or just drag its folder onto the GlyphViz window. Press **Space** to start the animation.
