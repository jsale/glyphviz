# AutoSleep — A Year of Sleep

A year of Apple Watch sleep data: one Rod-topology cylinder per month carrying a flattened disc per day, each day ringing its hours with Torus topology — sleep quality encoded in color.

![AutoSleep — A Year of Sleep loaded in GlyphViz](../assets/examples/autosleep-2025.png)

**Try it:** [download the examples set](../getting-started/installation.md#example-scenes), then open `AutoSleep_2025_Example/autosleep_2025_gv_node.csv` — or just drag its folder onto the GlyphViz window. Press **Space** to start the animation.
